§1About Me

I am Venkata Sai Varun Mooraboina, a data engineer at CFA Institute. My work centers on moving data between systems, keeping it accurate, and making it ready for analysis. I am also a student at Indiana Wesleyan University, where I am building a foundation in data analytics and machine learning.

This portfolio collects the work I complete in the Machine Learning Fundamentals course. Each artifact shows what I made, how I made it, and why it matters to the people who would use it. My goal is to grow from data engineering into roles where I help teams prepare reliable data for machine learning and explain the results to people who are not technical.

§2My Value Proposition

I am a data engineer who is building machine learning skills. I turn scattered, messy data into clean datasets that analysts and models can trust, and I explain what the results mean in plain language. I help data and machine learning teams spend less time fixing data and more time using it to make decisions.

  1. Data you can trust

    Scattered, messy data becomes clean datasets ready for analysts and models.

  2. Results in plain language

    I explain what the results mean to people who are not technical.

  3. Less fixing, more deciding

    Teams spend their time using data to make decisions, not repairing it.
